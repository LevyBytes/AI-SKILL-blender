# Video strips: Lock/Mute

### Lock/Mute

Lock Strips Ctrl - H
Disables movement of the strip.

Unlock Strips Ctrl - Alt - H
Re-enables movement.

Mute/Unmute Strips H , Alt - H
Mute or unmute the selected strips.

Mute/Unmute Deselected Strips Shift - H , Shift - Alt - H
Mute or unmute every strip except the selected strips.
